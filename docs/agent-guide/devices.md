# Devices: physical iPhones, preview and the device pool

*`quern://guide/devices` -- part of the Quern agent guide. Start from `quern://guide`.*

Getting a device, driving physical iPhones, watching devices live, and changing device settings.

This topic is split into pages, one per subject. Read the one you need:

- `quern://guide/devices/pool` -- Device Pool for Parallel Testing: ensure_devices and resolve_device, the active device, choosing a platform
- `quern://guide/devices/controlling` -- Controlling a device: booting, installing, launching, terminating, uninstalling and listing apps
- `quern://guide/devices/physical` -- Working with Physical Devices: physical iPhones: first-time WDA setup, and what works the same or differently than on a simulator
- `quern://guide/devices/physical-proxy` -- Proxy capture on a physical device: capturing a phone's traffic: Android in one call, an iPhone's Wi-Fi proxy and the CA step by step
- `quern://guide/devices/preview` -- Live Preview of Devices and Simulators: live video of a device or simulator: preview_device
- `quern://guide/devices/builds` -- Building and installing: build_and_install: Xcode schemes and Gradle projects, onto one or more devices
- `quern://guide/devices/checkpoints` -- Starting from a known state: app checkpoints and plists: simulator app checkpoints (save/restore_app_state, the keychain for logged-in states) and app plists
- `quern://guide/devices/settings` -- Device settings: text input, hardware keyboard, locale, location and permissions, hardware buttons, factory reset
- `quern://guide/devices/foldables` -- Foldable simulators: iPhone Duo simulators: set_hinge and get_hinge
- `quern://guide/devices/mistakes` -- Mistakes to avoid: hardcoding device UDIDs
- `quern://guide/devices/troubleshooting` -- When something is wrong: an empty screen summary on a physical device: WDA read budgets
